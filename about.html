On the historicGrand Trunk Road

A stay to remember

Hospitality, shaped around you.

For years, Rohit International has welcomed travellers, families and wedding parties to Sasaram with the warmth of a family home and the care of a good hotel.

We sit on the Old G.T. Road — the ancient highway Sher Shah Suri built from this very town — with the green hills of Kaimur on the horizon and the shrine of Maa Tarachandi close by. Whether you are here for a night on the road, a family occasion or the city's monuments, we will look after the details.
